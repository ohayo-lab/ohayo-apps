FAQ

Small questions, quick answers
I bought a monthly deck — will the Complete deck give me duplicates?

No. Monthly decks and the Complete deck are built with the same card IDs, so Anki recognizes cards you already own and merges them. Your review history stays intact — nothing is doubled, nothing is lost.

What devices does this work on?

Any device that runs Anki: Windows, Mac and Linux (free), Android via AnkiDroid (free), and iPhone/iPad via AnkiMobile (paid) or AnkiWeb in the browser (free). Import takes one tap, and AnkiWeb sync keeps all your devices in step.

Is the audio streamed, or does it work offline?

The audio is embedded inside every card. Once you import the deck, everything — sentences, translations and native audio — works completely offline. Perfect for commutes and flights.
