DAY 1 · JANUARY 7
Nothing Beats My Own Bed我が家のベッドが世界一しあわせ

This broadcast is being recorded on Wednesday, January 7, 2026.

I spent a slightly longer New Year's holiday at my parents' house this year.

The first thing I think about when I return to my home in Tokyo after a long time is how truly happy I am to sleep in my own bed.

I'm quite particular about sleeping, so I use my favorite bed and mattress.

In addition, if my room is cluttered, I can't focus on what's in front of me, so I try to keep my belongings to a minimum and create a simple, calming space.

By arranging my home to my liking in this way, I really feel at ease in my house.

Right after the New Year's holiday.

How are you all bringing your everyday life back to normal?

This is the first broadcast of 2026 and also the first one for OHAYO NIHONGO.

DAY 2 · JANUARY 8
The People Around You Change You周りの人で人生が変わる

Today, I'd like to share a little bit of my daily life.

Yesterday I was working at the shared office as usual, and what I realized again yesterday was that, even though this is the very first week of the new year, everyone around me was getting their work done briskly, as if they had no post-holiday fog at all, and that really inspired me.

When I work in that kind of environment, I feel my own motivation naturally rising too.

Once again, I thought about how very important it is where you are, what kind of environment you put yourself in, and what kind of people you surround yourself with as you live each day.

Then, on my lunch break, I made my first shrine visit of the year to the shrine of the local guardian deity near my office.

Since I spend New Year's at my parents' house every year, it's my yearly tradition to make my first shrine visit after coming back to Tokyo.

This year again, I safely received a paper talisman and charms, displayed them on the Shinto altar at home, and now I'm all set for the new year.

I'll keep sharing little daily happenings like this in Japanese, so please look forward to it.

DAY 3 · JANUARY 9
I Fell and Limped Home for 20 Min新年早々、捻挫した

Right at the start of the new year, I took a little tumble on my way home.

At first, I thought it wasn't a big deal, but when I stood up, my left ankle hurt a lot, and I ended up taking 20 minutes to get home, which usually only takes 7 or 8 minutes.

The next day, the pain still hadn't gone away, so I went to the hospital.

After taking an X-ray at the hospital, they found no major issues with the bone and diagnosed it as a mild sprain.

They told me to keep my left ankle supported so it doesn't move and to go about my daily life as long as it doesn't hurt.

I'm relieved it wasn't a serious injury.

I love traveling alone, and before COVID, I used to travel abroad 3 or 4 times a year.

I loved going to new places, meeting new people, crying and laughing, and having adventures I could never have in my everyday life.

But since COVID, even though I travel abroad for work every year, I feel like I hardly have any new adventures or exciting trips just for myself.

DAY 5 · JANUARY 11
The Workout I've Kept for 5 Years私にいちばん合っていた運動はピラティス

Today, for the first time in 2026, I went to a nail salon, got an eyelash perm, and did machine Pilates.

Machine Pilates is a really, really important workout that I've been doing for 5 years now.

I tried yoga and kickboxing too, but machine Pilates is a method used for rehabilitation in Europe, and it helps align the small muscles at the core of your body with tiny, tiny movements.

Even though I couldn't see the effects right away, as I kept going, I came to notice, 'Oh, so this is how the body gets aligned.'

Now, I have one-on-one lessons with my instructor twice a month, and this time is very, very precious to me.

I hope to continue this habit from now on.

Every time I get on the plane back to Japan, I think, "Ah, I want to eat Korean food again soon!"

DAY 11 · JANUARY 17
A Tip I Learned About My Smoke Alarmカーテンが火事の敵だなんて

This morning, there was a fire alarm inspection in the apartment building where I live.

It's done every year around this time, and the inspector came to my room to check if the fire alarm was working properly.

They told me then that it's better not to partition the room with curtains, for the fire alarm.

It seems that if the airflow is blocked, it becomes harder to detect smoke.

When a fire occurs, it affects not only your room but also the surroundings, so it's important to be careful every day.

I thought I'd like to be careful with things that use fire, like candles and incense, and stay safe.
