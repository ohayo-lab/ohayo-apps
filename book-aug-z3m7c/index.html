Every time I come to Thailand, I always do a homestay at my friend's house.

It's a rural town about 100 kilometers from the airport in Bangkok, a place with really nothing at all.

I usually live right in the middle of Tokyo, so the few days I spend here are a really quiet, calm, and comfortable time.

This time I spend with my friend and their family, as if I were living here, is my treasure.

I'm grateful that my bond with my Thai friend, whom I met more than 15 years ago, has continued for so long like this, even now.

It's short, so please try listening to it once more.

DAY 31 · AUGUST 31
I've Been Eating Thai Food All Day朝からタイ料理を食べ続けています

Today, I've been eating Thai food nonstop since the morning.

It's only natural since I'm in Thailand, but Thai food is just so delicious!

It's the authentic taste, completely different from the Thai food you eat in Japan, and my heart has been filled with delicious happiness the whole time.

I came to Thailand for the first time in about a year and a half, and I had completely forgotten how delicious it is.

Today's lunch was duck noodles.

I had ramen with duck meat in it, and satay, which is grilled pork on skewers.

Even though I just had lunch a moment ago and I'm full, I'm already looking forward to what tonight's dinner will be.
