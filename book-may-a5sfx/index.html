DAY 1 · MAY 1
Three Minutes of Radio Exercisesたった3分のラジオ体操で130か所の筋肉

Every morning when I head out for my walk, I see a couple doing radio exercises at the entrance of the apartment building.

In Japan, radio exercises are something we've done as a matter of course since childhood, at school and at neighborhood events.

Once I got to junior high and high school, they somehow started to feel silly and I only did them half-heartedly, but the three-minute routine is ingrained in my body, and my body still properly remembers both the music and the movements.

Today, inspired by that couple, I tried doing radio exercises too, in a park along my walk.

My body, which is usually cramped from not being used, stretches out, my back straightens up, and it feels really good.

When I looked it up, I found that radio exercises apparently include movements that work muscles in as many as 130 places.

I feel like just keeping it up for only three minutes every day could help me live a healthy life day to day.

DAY 2 · MAY 2
Tokyo Feels Empty This Golden Weekゴールデンウィークの東京は人が減って気持ちいい

During Golden Week, there are fewer people in the city, and I couldn't be happier.

I don't know whether the people who are usually in Tokyo are staying home or traveling, but anyway, there are fewer people around.

Apparently, these days the number of travelers from overseas has become larger than the number of people living in Tokyo.

The streets, which are usually packed with residents and tourists, seem to have just a little space now.

During this holiday, I'm planning not to travel anywhere and to spend my time near home.

Instead of traveling during the long holiday when prices are high, I'm thinking of taking time off at a different time and traveling then.

DAY 3 · MAY 3
A Sky That Looked About to Cry1日中、今にも泣き出しそうな空

Today, the sky was gloomy and overcast all day.

While I was working, I looked up at the sky from the window and kept thinking, "The sky looks like it's about to cry at any moment."

There are lots of cloudy days, but it's really rare for a sky that looks like it could start raining at any moment to last all day, like today.

Around the time I finished work and was heading home, the sky began to cry, drop by drop.

It wasn't enough rain to need an umbrella; it was really just drop by drop.

The rain stopped right away, but it's been a long time since I last thought something like, "The sky looks like it's about to cry."

Today, it seems the sky was holding back its tears.

DAY 4 · MAY 4
A Pink Sky After a Perfect Day欲張らない計画で半端ない達成感

Last night, I made a plan for today.

First thing in the morning, I went to the movie theater and watched a movie I had wanted to see.

Then I had lunch and worked through the things I wanted to do, in the order I had listed them.

I always have so many things I want to do that I end up making greedy plans without meaning to.

When I get too greedy, I end up staying up late at night or not being able to get up early in the morning, and then I don't finish as planned and I feel down.

So this time, I chose an amount I could definitely get through at my own pace.

Thanks to that, lots of things went really smoothly, and I feel an incredible sense of accomplishment.

On my way home, a beautiful pink sunset spread across the sky.

Thinking, "Ah, it was a good day," I felt calm and peaceful.

DAY 5 · MAY 5
I Discovered a Game Called Mockleモックルって知ってる?

A little while ago, on my way to go shopping, I passed by a park, and lots of people had gathered there for some kind of event.

When I took a peek, it turned out to be an event for a sport called Mölkky.

Do you know Mölkky?

It was my first time hearing about it, but apparently you line up wooden pieces, like building blocks about the size of a can of juice, on the sand, then throw a piece of wood at them to knock them down and score points.

Today the weather is neither too hot nor too cold, very pleasant for spending time outside, so everyone from children to elderly people was enjoying it together.

Watching it up close, I thought it looked really fun.

Kids, grandpas and grandmas all enjoying it together - isn't that just the best?

DAY 6 · MAY 6
Like a Trip to Korea in Tokyo新大久保でまるごと韓国を満喫

Yesterday, I went out to eat Korean food with a friend.

Shin-Okubo in Tokyo is a place called Koreatown, lined with Korean restaurants, Korean cosmetics shops, and even supermarkets selling Korean ingredients, so it really feels like you've come on a trip to Korea.

After eating Korean food, I always buy my favorite kimbap on the way home. It's my usual routine.

I buy it for the next day's breakfast.

This time, I stopped by a Korean supermarket on the way home and bought snacks and tea.

Then I stopped by a Korean cosmetics shop and bought a little more skincare to restock.

I'd like to go to Shin-Okubo again soon to eat Korean food.

DAY 7 · MAY 7
The New Station That Feels Like a Park木に囲まれた高輪ゲートウェイ駅

I stopped by Takanawa Gateway Station, the newest station in Tokyo.

It's a station designed by the architect Kengo Kuma, and it uses a lot of wood, so it's full of a natural feel and a sense of openness.

Since it opened in 2020, I've passed through it on the train a few times, but this was the first time I got off and stopped by.

The whole station is like a space where you can relax, and as soon as you step out of the ticket gates, there's a cafe that looks down over the tracks and an artificial grass area where children can play.

It wasn't like the closed-in train stations we've had until now, surrounded by concrete; it was full of openness and a really pleasant space.

If I can feel such a pleasant atmosphere just by stepping out of the ticket gates, I'd like to go there from now on to enjoy the station's spaces.

DAY 8 · MAY 8
Why Does Meryl Streep Never Age?メリル・ストリープが20年前と変わらない

During Golden Week, I saw "The Devil Wears Prada" at the movie theater.

The first film from 20 years ago was also really fun, and this one was fun too, full of energy.

What surprised me was that Meryl Streep's beauty had hardly changed from 20 years ago.

Anne Hathaway, who was in her twenties back then, is now 43, and she's beautiful with the expression of an adult who has lived through a lot, but I really wonder what the secret to Meryl Streep's beauty is.

Also, the way all the actors stood and walked was strikingly beautiful.

People at the very top of the world really are consummate professionals, aren't they?

What resonated with me at this point in my life was the attitude toward life and work.

Both movies and books teach us many things, depending on when we watch or read them.

It may be just entertainment, but it's more than just entertainment.

DAY 9 · MAY 9
New Glasses Made Life Comfortable Again老眼が進んだのでメガネを作り替えた

My eyesight isn't good, so I usually wear contact lenses, and at home I wear glasses.

After I passed 45, my age-related farsightedness started, and not being able to see my phone screen or the documents on my desk was unpleasant in itself.

After trying various things, I ended up with hard contact lenses that let me see both far and near, but they hurt when dust gets in, and I always feel like there's a lens in my eye.

So in the fall of 2024, I had glasses made that let me see both medium and close distances.

They were comfortable for watching TV, looking at my computer, and reading books at home, but recently my farsightedness seemed to have gotten a little worse again.

I went to the glasses shop and had them change the lenses to ones that match my current eyesight, and now I can be comfortable both at home and outside.

I'm really glad I had my glasses remade.

DAY 10 · MAY 10
Kinshicho Was Closer Than I Thought東京駅から3駅、初めての錦糸町

I've lived in Tokyo for more than 35 years, but there are still many neighborhoods I've never been to.

Yesterday, I went to a neighborhood called Kinshicho for the first time.

In my mind, it felt like a really faraway place, but it was only three stops from Tokyo Station, and I was surprised at how unexpectedly close it was.

When I got off at the station, the first thing that caught my eye was the big park in front of the station.

Then, buildings lined the area as if surrounding the station, and there was absolutely everything, from shopping malls to restaurants.

What I had imagined was more of an old downtown neighborhood, so I was shocked at how very urban it was.

I'll have more chances to go to Kinshicho for a while from now on, so I'm thinking of exploring the neighborhood a little.

DAY 11 · MAY 11
Why Hard Books Are Worth It難しい本ほど新しい世界の入り口

As usual, I was reading with an audiobook when I came across a new way of thinking.

Do you ever find a book difficult when you're reading it?

I often do.

Sometimes it's so difficult that I give up halfway, or I put it off and just leave it like that.

But apparently, that is a step into a new world.

Values you don't have inside you yet start from zero, so apparently it's natural to find them difficult.

On the other hand, apparently the things you can read smoothly and enjoyably are about a world you already know, because it has already become part of your knowledge, so you can read on easily.

I had never thought about it that way before, so I thought, "I see."

Thinking of it that way, I want to keep reading all kinds of books without overthinking, telling myself that of course they're difficult, because they're about things I don't know.

The Same Park Looks Different by Noon朝と昼で公園は全然違う表情

I sometimes pass through the same park twice a day.

The first time is during my early-morning walk before 6 a.m., and the second is when I go shopping at the supermarket after finishing my housework.

The morning scenery and the late-morning scenery are completely different, and that's what I enjoy.

In the morning, the park is quiet with no one around, and the sky still holds the feeling of the time before dawn.

By late morning, the sky grows stronger along with the sun's energy, and the trees and flowers look different depending on the time of day.

Yesterday, too, when I passed by the park just before noon, I found lots of lovely pink roses blooming that I hadn't noticed in the morning.

Beautiful leaves were growing healthily on the crape myrtle tree, and somehow that made me happy.

The sky, the air, the flowers and the trees all show a different face every day in step with the season, so that alone makes my heart excited and happy.

This morning, it started to drizzle partway through my walk.

After I got home, while I was doing the laundry and eating breakfast, the rain stopped, the sky turned blue, and it became a really refreshing sky.

I've made this morning exercise a habit for more than 10 years now, but strangely, the days when it was pouring and I couldn't go for my walk are so few I can count them.

For some reason, even when it's raining, the rain stops or turns into a drizzle just during this morning time.

Like today, whenever it drizzles during my morning walk, this mystery, one of my own "seven wonders," crosses my mind.

On my balcony there are still three daikon radishes and two turnips that I haven't harvested yet, so from now on I want to make sure I don't forget the morning watering.
