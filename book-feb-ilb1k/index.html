DAY 1 · FEBRUARY 1
I Always End Up Choosing Sushi疲れて帰った夜、お寿司のお弁当に救われる

Today I was busy with work all day from the morning, and by the time I went outside, it had gotten quite late.

I was a little tired, so on my way home I went to buy a bento box for dinner.

The shop had all kinds of bento boxes, and every one of them was lined up looking very delicious.

From among them, I chose a sushi bento that looked fresh and delicious.

Even though there are lots of other delicious-looking bentos, like hamburg steak, omurice and fried shrimp, I always end up choosing sushi.

When I got home, I took my time and savored it with a cup of hot tea.

With the fresh toppings and the lovely aroma of the sushi, it felt like my body and mind were gently melting.

When you eat a delicious meal, the tiredness of the day just disappears somewhere, doesn't it?

DAY 2 · FEBRUARY 2
I Can't Stop the Office Snacks会社のお菓子に毎日手が伸びてしまう

Today the sky was a little cloudy, but it was a very calm day.

In the break area at my office, there are always delicious-looking snacks.

There are all kinds, like chocolate, cookies, and rice crackers.

Sometimes I can't help eating a lot of them during work.

Sweet snacks gently ease my tiredness, so even though I think I shouldn't eat too much, I find myself reaching for them.

They say sweet things are good when you're tired, but eating too much isn't good, is it?

I always eat too much and then regret it.

DAY 3 · FEBRUARY 3
Beating the Sunday Night Bluesサザエさんが始まると週末が終わる憂鬱

In Japan, there's a TV anime show called Sazae-san that everyone knows.

It's a really long-running show that has continued for more than 55 years since it first aired.

It airs every Sunday evening at 6:30.

So when Sazae-san starts on Sunday, many people feel gloomy, thinking, "The weekend is going to be over."

I, too, can't help feeling a little heavy-hearted on Sunday nights.

So I decided to make one reward for myself on Sunday nights.

I have my favorite food for dinner, or take a slow, relaxing bath with my favorite bath salts.

Just by creating something to look forward to like this, my gloomy mood gets lighter.

Since it's a precious Sunday, it's nice to try finding ways not to feel gloomy, isn't it?

DAY 4 · FEBRUARY 4
I Wish I Owned 365 Towelsタオルが365枚あれば洗濯しなくていい

The weather was nice from the morning today, and it was a perfect day for doing laundry.

Every day, I love getting through my morning housework efficiently.

But there is just one chore that I find a little bit of a hassle.

It's hanging up the washed laundry one piece at a time.

On busy mornings, I can't help feeling that this time is such a waste.

Suddenly, I thought, "I wish I had 31 days' worth of towels and underwear."

That way, I wouldn't have to hang out laundry for a whole month, right?

But for a whole year, I would need as many as 365 of them.

That really is way too many, and imagining it, I ended up laughing to myself.

Seeing the laundry swaying under the blue sky feels really nice.

In the end, today too I hung it out as usual, and I felt refreshed.

DAY 5 · FEBRUARY 5
Chasing a Perfect Sleep ScoreApple Watchが教えてくれた私の睡眠

Recently, I've been using my Apple Watch to check my sleep.

Yesterday's sleep score was 88 points.

I'd like to aim for 90.

Actually, lately I've been bothered by how often I wake up in the middle of the night to go to the toilet.

I'm really happy on mornings when I haven't woken up even once during the night to go to the toilet.

So, to sleep more deeply, I decided to find ways to improve how I spend my evenings.

First, I changed the lights in my room to a warm color that helps me relax.

And I've stopped using my smartphone before bed, and I try to fall asleep while listening to an audiobook.

When I quietly listen to a story without using my eyes, my mind naturally calms down.

When I sleep soundly, the refreshed feeling when I wake up in the morning is completely different.

DAY 6 · FEBRUARY 6
Mac Mini or MacBook? I Can't DecideMac miniかMacBook Airで悩み中

Today I was thinking about a new computer.

Right now, I'm really torn over whether to buy a Mac mini or a MacBook Air.

I want to buy one soon, but both are so appealing that I just can't decide.

I'm surrounded by nothing but Apple gadgets, like my iPhone and iPad.

Right now, I use a MacBook Pro for work and an iPad Air for studying.

My watch, phone, and earphones are also all Apple gadgets.

Apple gadgets can all be connected and used together, so they're very convenient.

Agonizing over which model to choose is fun, but on the other hand, it's frustrating that time just keeps passing.

Choosing a new gadget should be something to look forward to, but I still can't decide, so I want to make up my mind soon and feel relieved.

DAY 7 · FEBRUARY 7
The Hand Cream That Saved My Hands韓国のハンドクリームで手がツルツル

It's a little chilly this morning, but the sunlight is gentle and it's a pleasant morning.

Recently, I suddenly started to worry about how dry and wrinkled my hands are.

So I started using a hand cream from a Korean brand called Tamburins.

This hand cream smells really good and puts me in a happy mood, so I buy it every time I go to Korea.

I tried applying it often every day and carefully kept moisturizing.

Then my hands, which had been dry and wrinkly, gradually became smooth.

I see my own hands all the time, so when they're smooth, that alone makes me grin.

From now on too, I'm going to keep moisturizing with hand cream and not slack off.

DAY 8 · FEBRUARY 8
Too Old to Eat All My Setsubun Beans54歳が食べる豆の数は9個

Last week, February 3rd was Setsubun (the traditional day before the start of spring), so I did the bean-throwing ritual at home.

It's an old Japanese custom where you throw beans while saying, "Demons, out! Good luck, in!"

It's said that if you eat as many beans as your age, you'll be healthy.

But I'm 54, so I can't eat that many beans.

So I added 5 and 4 and decided to eat nine beans.

I cheated a little, but with this, it looks like I'll be able to stay healthy this year too.

I want to keep cherishing customs like this, which let us feel the seasons, for a long time to come.

DAY 9 · FEBRUARY 9
A Huge Full Moon at Dawn朝の散歩で出会った大きな白い満月

This morning the sky was gloomy and overcast, and it was a little darker than usual.

I walk every morning, but today, for a change of mood, I headed in a different direction than usual.

Then, low in the sky, I saw a really big, perfectly round moon.

I was moved by the sight of the white moon quietly setting in the brightening sky.

I usually head toward the morning sun, but it's nice to see different scenery once in a while.

Getting to come across something so beautiful first thing in the morning made my heart feel very full.

It reminded me once again that this is also one of the joys of getting up early.

It looks like I'll be able to spend today, too, feeling calm and peaceful.

DAY 10 · FEBRUARY 10
Nervous at the Dentist, Then Praised歯医者さんに歯がきれいだと褒められた日

Yesterday afternoon, I went to the dentist feeling a little nervous.

I love eating, so I want to keep enjoying delicious meals with my own teeth forever.

Dental health affects the health of your whole body, so I'm always worried about whether I'm brushing my teeth properly every day.

But at this checkup, I was praised: "Your teeth and the inside of your mouth are both very clean."

I brush, floss, and use mouthwash every day without fail, so I was very relieved.

I felt once again that early care is important for protecting the health of your teeth.

I want to keep taking careful care of my teeth and cherish them for the rest of my life.

DAY 11 · FEBRUARY 11
My Yearly Body Check (Nervous!)1年に1度の健康診断、ドキドキの1日

Today, I went to my once-a-year health checkup.

Not just my height and weight, but everything inside my body was checked, including my heart activity and a stomach camera exam.

With food restrictions starting the day before and a busy schedule from the morning on the day itself, it was a very important day that comes only once a year.

I was a little nervous during the tests, but I was relieved when they were over without any problems.

Knowing your current physical condition well is necessary for spending each day calmly, isn't it?

Once again, I thought that I want to spend another year healthy and happy until next year's checkup.

Everyone, please take care of your health too, and enjoy every day.

DAY 12 · FEBRUARY 12
A Snowman With a Red Flower on Its Head東京に雪が積もって雪だるまを作る

On a snowy day last week, when I woke up in the morning and looked out the window, everything around was pure white.

It's rare for snow to pile up in Tokyo, so I went to the park feeling a little excited.

I took a break from my usual walk and decided to make a small snowman.

I made its arms out of tree branches, decorated its head with a red sasanqua flower, and turned it into a cute girl.

People passing by said, "How cute!" and took pictures, which made me very happy.

While I was absorbed in making it, forgetting the cold, I felt as if I had gone back to my childhood.

It was only a short time until it melted, but I found a special joy of snowy days.

It turned into a very happy day that warmed my heart.

DAY 13 · FEBRUARY 13
Someone Gave My Snowman a Face雪だるまに友達ができました

Today, I'll tell you what happened next with the snowman I made yesterday.

Since I had gone to the trouble of making a snowman, I wanted lots of people to see it, so I put it out in a place where many people pass by.

When I went back to check on it three hours later, to my surprise, the snowman had a cute face.

It seems someone had made its eyes and nose using twigs and acorns.

Even more surprisingly, two new snowman friends were lined up right next to it.

I don't know who did it or where they were from, but coming across that kindness made me feel very happy.

Snow in Tokyo melts away in a single day, but it was a wonderful day that left a warm memory in my heart.

It turned into a very happy experience that left my heart feeling satisfied.

DAY 14 · FEBRUARY 14
My Birthday, All to Myself誕生日はひとりで好きなウニを食べる日

My birthday is in February.

Every year, I make a point of taking time to treat myself.

This year I had work on my actual birthday, so on my way home from the office, I stopped by a bar in my neighborhood and enjoyed a drink.

The next evening, I went out for sushi, which I love, and ate lots of sea urchin, my absolute favorite.

Actually, I really love going out for drinks or meals by myself.

Eating while chatting with friends is fun too, but on my birthday, and only then, I've made it a rule to eat what I like alone, at my own pace.

It's a very luxurious time.

I ate plenty of my favorite things, my heart and my stomach were both full, and I felt so happy.

Until my birthday comes around again next year, I want to spend every day full of energy and with a big appetite.

DAY 15 · FEBRUARY 15
How a Simple Hello Changed My Day挨拶ひとつで職場が楽しくなる

I usually work at a shared office, a place where all kinds of people gather.

Every day I sit alone at my computer, but recently I've made a lovely friend I exchange greetings with.

Some time ago, when I was trying to decide whether to buy a new computer, I worked up the courage to speak to a woman right in front of me who was using exactly the computer I wanted.

When she was finishing work and getting ready to go home, I started the conversation myself, because I wanted her to tell me what it was like to use.

After that, we naturally started saying "Good morning" every time we saw each other.

Until then, I had been working in silence, but just exchanging a single greeting makes me feel so much brighter.

With just one more smile, work time also becomes calmer and more enjoyable, doesn't it?

I felt once again that a greeting is like a magic word that connects people.

DAY 16 · FEBRUARY 16
Spring Bloomed Inside My Room部屋の中にも春、エスキナンサスの赤い花

It's still cold outside, but spring is gradually arriving inside my room.

I have lots of houseplants at home, and right now, my Aeschynanthus has started to bloom.

It's a variety called Mona Lisa, and it gives me lots of deep red flowers shaped like little bells.

Every year, before I know it, the flowers are blooming, and just looking at them gives me energy.

My days are busy, but time spent gazing at beautiful flowers makes the heart feel very calm, doesn't it?

Finding a little spring inside my room made me feel very happy today.

The time I spend growing plants is an important time for me to relax.

DAY 17 · FEBRUARY 17
The Tool I Forgot I Owned忘れていた蒸し器が想像以上の活躍

Today, I went to a hair salon to get my hair cut.

While I was chatting with the hairstylist, I remembered the steamer I had completely forgotten about lately.

Right away, this evening, I used that steamer to steam vegetables, fish and meat, and ate them.

When you use a steamer, the fat from the meat and fish drips off, and the nutrients in the vegetables stay in, so it's very healthy.

It brought out the natural flavor of the ingredients, and it was more delicious than I had imagined, so I was very satisfied.

It had been sleeping at the bottom of my memory, but if it's this convenient, I think I'll keep making good use of the steamer from now on.

The warm meal filled both my heart and my body, and it turned into a very peaceful evening.

DAY 18 · FEBRUARY 18
My Clinic Moved, but Nothing Changed場所は変わっても中の作りが全く同じ

When February comes, pollen from Japanese cedar trees starts flying in Japan.

I have terrible hay fever, so every year around this time I go to an ear, nose and throat clinic and get medicine.

The doctor at the clinic I go to is very straightforward and explains things clearly, so I really love this doctor.

Actually, the clinic recently moved to a new location, and when I went there, I was really surprised.

The reception, the waiting room, the sofas and so on were arranged exactly the same way as at the old clinic.

I felt completely bewildered, as if a fox had played a trick on me, but I sensed the doctor's commitment to efficiency, never wasting thought on unnecessary things, and I couldn't help laughing a little.

I felt I had caught a glimpse of the doctor's unwavering character, and I thought, "I'd like to be like that too."

Hay fever is tough, but since I get to see my favorite doctor, I'm just a little bit looking forward to my clinic visits at this time of year.

DAY 19 · FEBRUARY 19
Goodbye to the Clear Winter Sky気温18度、青い冬の空とお別れ

Even though it snowed just the other day, today it's as warm as 18 degrees, so I was very surprised.

Drawn out by the warm, spring-like sunshine, I went for a walk in a nearby park on my lunch break.

At the park, there were lots of people enjoying the signs of spring, and everyone was laughing happily.

But when I happened to look up at the sky, it wasn't the blue winter sky I love so much.

When the temperature rises, the sky starts to look a little white and hazy.

This is called harugasumi, or 'spring haze', a word that announces the arrival of spring.

I'm happy that it's getting warmer, but saying goodbye to the clear winter sky makes me a little sad.

When the seasons change, I always have mixed feelings, somewhere between happy and sad.

Today, at the park, I was able to slowly feel the season changing.

DAY 20 · FEBRUARY 20
The Food Foreigners Call 'Boring'納豆と同じものがアジアにあるなんて

Today, I learned a secret about natto, which I had always thought was a food found only in Japan, and I was very surprised.

I eat natto every day, but apparently there are actually similar foods in various countries in Asia.

While I was listening to the radio, I learned that in Myanmar and Thailand too, people eat fermented soybeans, just like natto.

In Japan, we mix it with soy sauce and put it on rice, but apparently in other countries it's used as a secret ingredient in cooking.

Apparently, from the point of view of people from other countries, the Japanese way of eating it is a little monotonous and boring.

Once again, I felt that there are still so many things in the world that I don't know.

From now on, I'd like to try using natto as a secret ingredient in cooking and discover new kinds of deliciousness.

Well, that's it for today.

See you tomorrow.

DAY 21 · FEBRUARY 21
A Fever Knocked Me Out for a Dayワクチン2回目で38度6分の高熱

I got a vaccine at the hospital yesterday, so today I have a high fever and I'm lying in bed at home.

It was a vaccine to prevent a disease called shingles, and this time it was my second shot.

I also got a fever after the first one, so I was a little worried, and sure enough, it went up to 38.6 degrees.

My body feels very hot and sluggish, but I'm really glad it's my day off from work.

Today I'd like to take it easy in bed all day and wait for my strength to come back.

From now on, I'll be less likely to get shingles, so for now I'll do my best to rest.

DAY 22 · FEBRUARY 22
Each of My Friends Shapes Me違うタイプの友達から元気をもらう

Recently, I met up with a friend I hadn't seen in a while, and we chatted a lot.

I only see this friend about once every few months, but this friend is a very important person to me.

Come to think of it, strangely enough, my friends all have completely different ways of living and thinking, and they each play a different role for me.

There are friends whose way of life I can relate to, and friends I can happily talk with about anything.

There are also wonderful friends who steadily build up their efforts, keep opening up new paths one after another, and show me how to go about my work and what really matters.

I get both inspiration and reassurance from friends of completely different types, and I feel very fortunate.

I took another look at myself, wondering whether I, too, am having some kind of good influence on my friends in the same way, and whether I'm doing okay.

DAY 23 · FEBRUARY 23
I Booked the Wrong Day, They Helped予約を間違えた私を許してくれた先生

This is about the time the other day when I went to the clinic at my appointment time to get a shingles vaccine.

At the reception desk, I was told that I didn't have an appointment, and I got really flustered.

Normally, I think they would say, "We can't do it today. Please come on the day of your appointment."

But that clinic responded differently, and they kindly told me, "It must have been our mistake. It's all right."

Thanks to that, I was able to get the vaccine that day without any problems.

When I got home and looked back at the confirmation email, it turned out I was the one who had made the booking wrong.

Even though I was the one who made the mistake, the doctor at the clinic didn't blame me at all and just helped me without any fuss, and I was really moved.

I thought I'd like to become a woman who can respond in such a calm and lovely way too.

DAY 24 · FEBRUARY 24
My Doctor Looks Just Like Kewpieキューピー人形そっくりのお医者さん

Because of the after-effects of the shingles I had last year, I'm still going to the hospital regularly.

I want to be free from the pain even one day sooner and 'graduate' from the hospital, but actually, there's just one thing I enjoy about going there.

It's that my doctor looks just like Kewpie, the mayonnaise character.

His hair is a little curly, and his eyes are big and bright.

He has a cute face, as if Kewpie had simply grown into a middle-aged man.

While I'm being examined, I always think, "He looks like Kewpie," and giggle to myself inside.

I can't help imagining that when the doctor was a child, he must have been as cute as the real Kewpie.

Of course, since I'm the only one being examined, this is my own little secret pleasure.

Feeling soothed by the doctor's gentle manner, I dream of the day I can fully recover soon and graduate from the hospital.

DAY 25 · FEBRUARY 25
Korea's Red Spice Isn't That Spicy韓国の粉唐辛子なしでは生きていけない

I love Korean food, so lately I've become completely hooked on Korean chili powder.

When I had soup in Korea, I found it strange that even though it looked bright red, it wasn't as spicy as I imagined.

Rather than spicy, it has a slight sweetness and strong umami, a flavor that's addictive.

I got curious about what made it so delicious, and when I looked it up, I found out it was Korean chili powder.

You can use it in any kind of dish, like kimchi or stir-fries, so I buy Korean chili powder at an imported food store and use it all the time.

I especially love combining it with soy milk, and I often make rice porridge or pasta with it.

I just thought of this, but it would probably be tasty to try it when making pickles with daikon radish too, wouldn't it?

Some red Korean dishes really are spicy, so you need to be careful, but it looks like this personal craze of mine will continue for a while yet.

DAY 26 · FEBRUARY 26
Why I Pretend to Be a Foreignerまた外国人に間違えられたから知らないふり

Lately, when I go to Ginza, it's full of foreigners and really crowded, and I get a little tired.

Japan right now is overflowing with tourists from abroad, and in some places there are more foreigners than Japanese people.

I'm 168 cm tall, which is on the tall side for a Japanese person, so for years people have often spoken to me in English.

But with the recent boom in travel to Japan, I've ended up being spoken to in English every single time I walk into a shop.

Every time a shop clerk said to me, "This is made in Japan," I used to answer, "I'm Japanese," but explaining it every time has become a bit of a hassle.

So lately, I've more often just gone along with it and pretended to be a foreigner.

In a way, it's like a little game I play by myself.

I'm sure there must be other people besides me who have had similar experiences.

DAY 27 · FEBRUARY 27
I Washed My Money at a Shrine小網神社で1万円から1円まで全部洗う

The other day, I went to pray at Koami Shrine in Nihonbashi, Tokyo.

Koami Shrine is very famous for its deity of financial fortune, and it has a custom called zeni-arai, where you purify money with water.

Purified money becomes 'seed money' that brings good luck, so apparently your luck improves if you keep it in your wallet or use it for good things.

Since I was there anyway, I put every kind of Japanese money, from a 10,000-yen bill down to a 1-yen coin, in a basket and washed it.

That was one 10,000-yen bill, one 5,000-yen bill, two 1,000-yen bills, and every coin from 500 yen down to 1 yen.

As I purified the bills and coins one by one, I somehow felt refreshed, as if even my own heart was being washed.

Of the money I washed this time, I decided to keep one 1,000-yen bill carefully in my wallet as a good-luck charm.

And I'm planning not to just spend the rest of the money, but to use it on things that will benefit my future self, like study costs.

I think spending money on my own growth will surely lead to the best financial luck.

DAY 28 · FEBRUARY 28
One Hour That Resets My Whole Bodyたった1時間で体がシャキッとするピラティス

Today, I went to a machine Pilates lesson, and my body feels refreshed and energized.

I feel that machine Pilates suits me better than any other exercise I've done so far, and I've already kept it up for more than five years.

In just a one-hour lesson, my posture, thrown off by things like computer work, gets neatly corrected.

Every time I finish a lesson, I think it's amazing that my body can change this much in one hour, and I can't help being moved.

At the same time, I looked back on my own life, thinking that if I can change my body in one hour, there must be lots of other things I can do too.

Like ending up lazing around staring at my phone without meaning to, or wasting time because I can't focus on one thing.

Time is limited, so from now on, I'd like to value my time more and be more mindful of how I spend it.
