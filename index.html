The morning loop

Three small moves. Sixty seconds.

The same tiny ritual every morning — not a course to finish, a habit to keep.

01

Play today’s line at a slow, natural pace. Let the sound land before the meaning.

Say it back, close behind the voice. Copy the melody, not just the words.
