The weather forecast says it will rain again tomorrow morning, but I'm looking forward to the sweet scent of the Somei Yoshino, so I'm thinking of going for a walk with my umbrella.

Isn't there some good way? I'm still searching for one, day after day.

My First Smoothie Was Too Cold冷たいスムージーで朝からトイレ通い

It's gotten quite a bit warmer, so this morning I made a smoothie for the first time in a while.

It has bananas, blueberries, mango, spinach, yogurt and soy milk.

During the summer, this is my usual breakfast. If I divide it into single portions and freeze them, I save the trouble of cutting fruit every morning, and it's good for my health too, so I like it.

But it seems a cold smoothie was still too early for this season, and my body got chilled.

I've been going to the bathroom again and again since this morning.

All through the winter, my breakfast was always warm rice porridge, so it seems my body was surprised by the sudden cold breakfast of vegetables and fruit.

I want to get my body used to it little by little.

DAY 29 · APRIL 29
Pasta and Wine on My Balconyベランダでパスタと白ワインの週末

This morning, the weather was really refreshing.

As usual, after spending the morning at a cafe, I was thinking of buying lunch and eating it in the park, but then I suddenly reconsidered and decided to make pasta at home and eat it on my balcony with white wine.

I spent money on coffee in the morning, so I'm saving on lunch.

Going home first, making a bento (packed lunch), and then going to the park wastes time, but on the other hand, buying lunch on the way back from the cafe and eating it in the park every weekend makes the expenses add up.

Last year, the pain from my shingles was severe almost all year round, and I had completely forgotten how pleasant it is to spend time on the balcony at my home.

Oh, that's right!

Come to think of it, I remembered that I liked enjoying this kind of time on the balcony.

Just like with work and study, the way you spend money needs a good balance between spending and saving, too.

Today I was able to remember an enjoyable way to spend my time, and it turned out to be a good day.

DAY 30 · APRIL 30
Giving My Old Towels a Second Lifeバスタオル3枚分の雑巾で大掃除

Today, I took care of something I had been putting off for a while.

I cut my worn-out bath towels into small pieces to use as cleaning rags.

It's not a big deal, but towels shed a lot of lint when you cut them, and bath towels are big, so it somehow felt like a hassle, and they had been sitting in a corner of my closet all this time.

Every year at New Year's, I replace my bath towels with new ones, and since the old ones are too good to waste, I cut them into small pieces and use them as table-wiping cloths and cleaning rags.

I can use them for cleaning the floor, the balcony railing, and the kitchen, so they still have plenty of use left in them.

Today, I made three bath towels' worth of cleaning rags.

I'm going to use these unhemmed rags generously and clean my room often.
